# Radios

### People Interested:

### Description:
Radios is a small "library-like" mod that allows for transmissions between two or more radios.

Radios has the ability to do actions on "non-discovered" structures that could have radios in.

for instance, a spacecraft could use a radio chanel and send a message to a generic "weather" chanel and get the
location of a weather station and of course, the weather.

Radios can also be used for lore (see [[Player Independent Events]]), Quests and Events.
