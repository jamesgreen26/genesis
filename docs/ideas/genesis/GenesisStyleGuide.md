# Genesis Mod Style Guide

## Technology

The style of the Genesis mod aims for dark sci-fi with 60s-70s, Soviet-style technology. Think a sci-fi moon landing if it were dark and foreboding.

### Zero Point Systems
Zero Point Systems is an interstellar corporation that had a massive influence over everything in the solar system before falling at the hands of rogue AI.

Color Guide:
- Dark Grey: Main body color, backgrounds, etc.
- Light Grey / White: Secondary colors, bridge colors
- Green/Blue: Accent colors

Logo: Sierpinski Carpet

### Zero Point Labs

Zero Point Labs is a subsidiary of ZPS that is in charge of developing the technology and periphery for space ships.

Color Guide: same as ZPS

Logo: (logo from ZPL mod page)
<br /><br /><br />
In all Zero Point tech mods, blocks with green accents are power generators, and blocks with blue accents are power consumers.


Existing media with similar atmospheres to the Genesis project’s goal:
- The Alien films
- 2001: A Space Odyssey (but with black as the main color and white as the accent color)
- Deep Rock Galactic (but with blue and green accents instead of other colors)
- The Matrix
- Lethal Company
- Blade Runner (but more 60s instead of 80s)
